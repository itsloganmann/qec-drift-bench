Failure mode taxonomy

How runs and failures are counted

Each scored failure has one primary L3 label under one L2 class and one L1 area. Passes and external interruptions have separate counts. A trace can show several failed checks for one episode.

The scoring rule determines joint pass rates. The score-aware trajectory review supplies a separate label. It can disagree with the rule. Review labels describe recorded evidence.

Historical reviews used rendered inputs that sometimes shortened long outputs. The replication reviews captured Codex output without middle cuts. One budget-stop archive has a possible unverified tail.

How evidence is linked

Source-verified excerpts were checked against saved records. Related excerpts are literal matches for terms relevant to the selected check. The timeline orders events and ends with the scorer or eligibility verdict. A missing match leaves the verdict and its review visible.
