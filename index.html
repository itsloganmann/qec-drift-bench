Quantum error correction

When the noise changes, can an AI agent repair the decoder?

QEC-Drift gives an agent a simulated quantum circuit and samples from before and after a noise change. It tests whether the agent can diagnose the change and write a decoder that works on fresh measurements.

Explore the benchmark ↓ Read the paper ↗

Logan Mann and Pranav Reddy · UC Santa Barbara

Benchmark design

Diagnosis and recovery are scored separately.

Inspect the circuit

The agent gets a baseline Stim circuit and detector measurements from before and after a possible change.

Diagnose and decode

The agent describes the noise change and writes a decoder that predicts which runs have logical errors.

Test on fresh shots

The grader checks the diagnosis and measures logical error reduction on held-out shots against reference decoders.
