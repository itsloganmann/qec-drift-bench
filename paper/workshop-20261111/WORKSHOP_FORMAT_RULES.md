# Workshop format check

Checked against organizer/conference sources on 11 November 2026. Workshop limits are set by each workshop and track, not uniformly by the parent conference. This is a named circulation preprint, not an anonymized submission or an acceptance claim.

| Example workshop | Main-text rule | References and appendix | Required submission format |
|---|---|---|---|
| [AISciK: AI & Science, NeurIPS 2026](https://aiscik.github.io/call-for-papers/) | 4-page or 8-page option | Outside the main-text count; unlimited. Appendix follows bibliography in the same PDF. Reviewers need only read main text. | NeurIPS2026 style or formatting that matches it. |
| [AI for Science, ICML 2026](https://ai4sciencecommunity.github.io/icml26.html) | Research and position tracks: 4-8 pages | Unlimited references and appendices. | ICML2026 style; double-blind submission. |
| [AI4Research, ICML 2026](https://ai4research-icml-workshop.github.io/) | Up to4pages | References excluded; supplementary material may follow references in the same PDF. | ICML2026 style; anonymized submission. |
| [AI&PDE, ICLR 2026](https://sites.google.com/impatech.edu.br/ai-pde) | Full papers: up to9pages; tiny papers:2-4pages | Main-text count excludes both; unlimited. | ICLR2026 template. |

Decision for this revision: four pages of main text, then a bibliography on a separate page, then an appendix. The official ICML2026 style is used in preprint mode. No font, margin, or line-spacing changes were made to force the main text into the limit. Detailed methods, full result tables, and audit qualifications are in the appendix; essential results and limitations remain in the main text.

Four main pages fit the length of the checked4-page options. This does not establish topic fit, a deadline, anonymity compliance, author eligibility, or compliance with a different workshop's style. For example, the NeurIPS and ICLR examples require their own formatting, and AISciK has separate AI-use eligibility and disclosure rules. No submission was made. The title page includes names and contact information because Logan requested a version to circulate.

Official ICML2026 style source: [conference style archive](https://media.icml.cc/Conferences/ICML2026/Styles/icml2026.zip), linked by the [conference call](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers). The bundled `.sty` and `.bst` are unmodified upstream dependencies; their hashes are recorded in `evidence/format-and-author-provenance.json`.
