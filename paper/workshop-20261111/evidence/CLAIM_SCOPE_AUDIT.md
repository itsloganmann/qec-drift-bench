**Verdict: INCONCLUSIVE**

Scope: an independent, generalizable claim about frontier-model scientific ability or causal failure mechanisms. The present corpus cannot establish that claim. The manuscript instead reports bounded observations about its recorded development episodes and a separate within-family replication.

| Evaluation element | Evidence and boundary |
|---|---|
| System under test | Logged model/effort configurations and heterogeneous API/CLI scaffolds. Backend weight revisions were not pinned. |
| Dataset and selection | The 23-task development core expanded after interim results. Fresh locations/seeds stay within selected templates and families. |
| Designer | The benchmark designer selected faults, references, task additions, thresholds and resource policies. |
| Ground truth | Stim-generated observables and planted noise structure support measured outcomes under the simulator. They do not establish fidelity to all physical hardware noise. |
| Scorer | Frozen diagnosis and logical-recovery criteria. An independent Parquet recomputation reproduced all 533 current relative verdicts. |
| Transcript evaluator | GPT-6-Astra, medium effort, exact-key protocols. Partially masked identifiers; score facts remain visible. No blinded expert label set. |
| Subject and dependence | Episodes share task templates and configurations. They are not independent binomial trials. |

Fired findings and their implications:

1. **Selection and split dependence.** Development difficulty was adapted after observing model outcomes. Fresh seeds are within-family. Generalization to unseen families is unresolved. Minimal fix: a separately frozen unseen-family set with matched repeated configurations. Residual risk: a finite synthetic grammar still limits hardware transfer.
2. **Evaluator-designer and scorer-label coupling.** The transcript evaluator sees the scoring facts and a designer-specified rubric. High agreement is not independent accuracy. Four current labels contradict measured repair floors; 47 historical labels dispute an eligibility policy. Minimal fix: blinded expert adjudication of a stratified sample, with score consistency checked separately. Residual risk: experts may disagree about explanations even after measured outcomes are settled.
3. **Unequal execution and availability.** Sparse fresh model coverage was credential-censored, and scaffold exposure differs. Rankings and causal effort effects are unsupported. Minimal fix: matched repeated runs under uniform resources and independently verified isolation. Residual risk: provider revisions and runtime variation remain.
4. **Historical linkage and heuristic subtypes.** Historical rows lack attempt IDs; damaged archives and later fresh source snapshots limit trace attribution. Decoder subtypes also rely on code heuristics. Minimal fix: atomic attempt-linked archives for all new runs and independent behavioral tests for decoder identity. Residual risk: identical outputs can still conceal different internal strategies.
5. **No controlled causal intervention.** Inspected validation, dispatch and localization defects motivate hypotheses; their corpus prevalence and causal effects were not measured. Minimal fix: preregistered, matched interventions on validation shapes, typed physical-ID dispatch, fallback submission and localization calibration. Residual risk: improvements can be task-specific.

The paper discloses each limitation and does not use the unestablished claims above as a conclusion. This audit does not certify scientific acceptance or a general performance ranking.
